How this actually works

Tiny Sports is an Australian not for profit. We apply for government sport grants on behalf of grassroots clubs and independent coaches who cannot apply for themselves, then hand the funding straight to them. This collection funds that work.

1

You tell us what you would wear. No payment, no commitment, no email required.

2

When enough people want the same thing, we place a batch with FRACTEL. Their minimum is 25 of a style, so this only happens if the interest is real.

3

FRACTEL makes it. That takes 8 to 10 weeks, so be realistic about timing. Stickers are made locally and arrive far sooner.

4

Every dollar of margin goes to the Tiny Sports grant program, and you get told which club it went to.

Made with FRACTEL, an Australian running brand, so their logo appears alongside the Tiny Sports mark on the finished garment. Designed by SAROGraphics.
