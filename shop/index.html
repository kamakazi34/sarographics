How this works

The Tiny Sports Collection is designed and sold by SAROGraphics as a fundraiser, with proceeds going to Tiny Sports Limited, an Australian not-for-profit that helps grassroots sports clubs and coaches get the grant funding that helps them grow.

Tapping Coming Soon on a product tells us there is real demand for it, which is exactly how we size the first batch. FRACTEL's minimum is 25 of a style, so a product only gets made if enough people want it.

Be realistic about timing. Once a batch is placed, FRACTEL takes 8 to 10 weeks to make and deliver it. Stickers are made locally and arrive far sooner. Questions: get in touch.
