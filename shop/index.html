How this works

The Tiny Sports Collection is designed and sold by SAROGraphics as a fundraiser, with proceeds going to Tiny Sports Limited, an Australian not-for-profit that helps grassroots sports clubs and coaches get the grant funding that helps them grow.

The shop opens very soon. Tapping Coming Soon on a product tells us there is real demand for it, which is exactly how we size the first small batch. Apparel ships 3 to 4 weeks after a batch is placed; stickers ship straight away once we are live. Questions: get in touch.
